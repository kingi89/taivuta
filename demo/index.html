String Template Formatter (format)

Syntax: {$variable:case[:role]}. Optional :role (person or place) disambiguates homonyms like Salo (surname vs. municipality).

Account Registration & Name Holder Preference Flow

In Finnish, certain personal names are morphologically ambiguous (for example, one person named Satu prefers Sadulle, while another prefers Satulle). Under Finnish linguistic conventions, the name holder themselves is the ultimate authority on their preferred inflection. taivuta supports capturing this preference during user registration, but if your application doesn't collect preferences, our automated heuristics supply safe linguistic defaults out of the box.
